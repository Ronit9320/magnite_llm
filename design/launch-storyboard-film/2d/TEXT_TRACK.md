# On-screen text track (revision to the storyboard)

The storyboard ran with no text except three bare chapter verbs (Choose, Bring, Make). Without
voiceover the viewer could not tell what the cables meant or what the product was until the end card,
and a lone verb like "Bring" read as a riddle. This track fixes both: a hook line in the first second,
a question that the silence answers, the name early, and numbered chapters with full phrases.

Rules
- Act I lines are cold grey-blue (#C3D2E4). Everything after the cut is paper (#F1EFE7).
- Chapter numbers are grey until that step is done, then turn orange (orange = done).
- The name is always wordmark.svg (rasterised from the SVG, never retyped).
- No claim that it never touches the internet. Only "Your prompts stay here." in S12.
- Text is screen-locked (parented to the camera), so it reads as a title, not as an object in the scene.

Other changes
- S01: notification pings start in the first second instead of at 1.3 s, so the opening moves at once.
- S04: instead of ending in a macro on the centre hole, the camera lifts at 2.6 s so the name has clean dark space.
- Panels in S06/S08 (and S10) are slightly smaller to leave room for the caption.
